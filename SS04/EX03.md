## 1. DIKW Architecture for Smart Orchid Farm

### Data
- Raw sensor readings: soil moisture, temperature, humidity, light.
- Stored as time-series logs: 50 MB/day.

### Information
- Processed data: averages, thresholds, alerts.
- Example: "Temperature > 35°C at 2 PM", "Soil moisture low".

### Knowledge
- Automation rules:
  - If temp > 35°C → turn on pump.
  - If soil moisture < 40% → irrigate for 5 minutes.
- Run rules on edge SoC to reduce latency.

### Wisdom
- Long-term optimization:
  - Use weather forecast to skip watering before rain.
  - Adjust schedule by season, crop stage, water usage.
  - Save water and reduce cost.
